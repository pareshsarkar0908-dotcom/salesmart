Amazon A+ Content: what it is and how to use it in India.

A+ Content turns a plain product page into a visual story. Used well, it lifts conversion and cuts returns - here is what it is, who can use it, and how to build modules that sell.

What A+ Content actually is

A+ Content lets brand-registered sellers replace the plain description with rich modules - image-and-text blocks, comparison charts, and banners - that explain the product visually below the buy box.

Who can use it

You need Amazon Brand Registry, which usually requires a registered trademark. Once enrolled, A+ becomes available for your brand's ASINs in Seller Central.

Modules that convert

Hero banner with core benefitBenefit + image blocksComparison chart across your rangeHow-to-use / setupMaterials and careBrand story and trust

Keep keywords out of A+, in your listing

A+ text is generally not indexed by Amazon search, so your ranking keywords still belong in the title, bullets, and backend terms. Build those with the SEO Keywords tool and the Amazon listing generator.

Use A+ to answer objections

Turn your most common buyer doubts into modules: a size chart for fit worries, a materials block for durability, a comparison chart for "which one do I pick". Find those objections fast with the review analyzer.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use A+ Content?

Brand Registry sellers and vendors - usually requires a registered trademark to enrol your brand.

Does A+ improve sales?

Often yes, via clearer benefits and fewer returns, though results vary by product and how well modules answer objections.

Is A+ indexed for search?

Generally not - keep keywords in title, bullets, and backend terms; use A+ for persuasion.

Where do I create it?

In Seller Central under A+ Content Manager, once your brand is enrolled.
