How to handle negative reviews on Amazon and Flipkart.

A negative review feels personal, but handled well it can build trust and hand you a free fix list. Panic and fake reviews only make things worse.

Stay calm and respond professionally

Reply publicly, politely, and briefly: acknowledge the issue, explain the fix, and offer to help. Future buyers read your response and judge whether you stand behind your product.

Fix the root cause, not just the review

One angry review is noise; a pattern is signal. If several buyers mention the same problem, the fix is operational or listing-level - not a reply. Spot the pattern with the review analyzer.

Turn complaints into listing updates

Size complaints -> add measurements + scale imageDurability doubts -> clarify material and usePackaging issues -> improve packagingConfusion -> add an FAQWrong expectations -> honest imagesMissing info -> complete the specs

What not to do

Never buy fake positive reviews or attack the reviewer - both risk suspension and destroy trust. You cannot remove genuine reviews; you can report ones that break marketplace policy and resolve real issues directly.

Close the loop on the listing

After fixing the cause, update the title, bullets, images, and FAQ so the same complaint stops recurring, then re-check with the Listing Score tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove a negative review?

Not genuine ones. You can report policy-violating reviews and resolve the buyer's issue, which sometimes leads to an update.

Should I respond?

Yes - calmly and professionally; future buyers read your response.

How do they affect sales?

They lower conversion and can hurt ranking, but patterns are free feedback to fix.

Are fake reviews worth it?

No - they risk suspension and destroy trust. Earn genuine ones instead.
