How to write product titles that rank and convert.

The title is the single most important line on your listing - it drives ranking and the click. Get it right with a simple, repeatable formula.

The title formula

Brand + Product Type + Key Feature/Material + Size/Quantity + Use Case/Benefit. Front-load the words buyers actually search, and keep it readable for a human, not just the algorithm.

Front-load the important words

The first 60-80 characters show in search and on mobile, where most Indian buyers shop. Put the product type and primary keyword there - never bury them behind filler like "premium" or "best".

Good vs weak titles

Weak: Best Quality Bottle PremiumGood: Steel Water Bottle 750ml, Insulated, Leak-ProofWeak: Cotton Kurti NiceGood: Cotton Straight Kurti for Women, Daily Wear, BlueWeak: Lunch Box SetGood: Steel Lunch Box for Office, 3 Containers, Leak-Resistant

Don't stuff keywords

Repeating the same phrase looks spammy and does not help ranking. Use one clear primary phrase, then spread variations across bullets and backend terms - build them with the SEO Keywords tool.

Adapt per marketplace

Amazon titles can be longer and detailed; Flipkart and Meesho titles should be shorter and plainer. Keep facts identical but adjust length and tone. Generate marketplace-specific versions with the Listing Generator.

Frequently asked questions

Brand + Product + Feature/Material + Size/Quantity + Use Case, with key words near the front.

How long should it be?

Within the marketplace limit, but the first 60-80 characters matter most - front-load them.

Repeat keywords?

No - one clear primary phrase; variations go in bullets and backend terms.

Same title everywhere?

Same facts, but adjust length and tone per marketplace.
