Ecommerce product photography tips that increase sales.

Your first image decides the click. On mobile-first marketplaces, better photos beat better copy for grabbing attention - and they cut returns by setting honest expectations.

Get the main image right

Use a pure white background, fill most of the frame, and show the product clearly with no text, logos, or props. This is a hard requirement on most Indian marketplaces and the image buyers see in search.

Light it well - natural light works

Soft, even light near a window beats a dark room and harsh flash. Avoid strong shadows and colour casts. Consistent lighting across a catalogue looks professional and trustworthy.

Shoot the angles buyers ask about

Show scale to cut returns

"Smaller than expected" is a top complaint and refund reason. A scale shot - the item in a hand or beside a common object - sets honest expectations and reduces returns.

Optimise for mobile

Most buyers shop on phones, so details must be readable on a small screen. Keep the subject large, avoid tiny text in images, and export at the marketplace's minimum resolution (often 1000x1000px or higher).

Match photos to your copy

Every objection you answer in text should have a matching image. Find the objections fast with the review analyzer, then plan an image for each and describe them using the Listing Generator.

Frequently asked questions

What should the main image be?

A clear product on pure white filling most of the frame, no text or props. Most marketplaces require it.

Can I shoot on a phone?

Yes - good natural light, clean background, clean lens, no zoom, highest resolution gives marketplace-quality images.

How many photos do I need?

White main image plus scale, use-case, detail, and packaging. More useful angles reduce doubt and returns.

Do photos affect ranking?

Indirectly - better photos raise click-through and conversion, and marketplaces reward listings that convert.
