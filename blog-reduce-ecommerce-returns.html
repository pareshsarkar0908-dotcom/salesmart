How to reduce ecommerce returns in India.

Returns quietly eat your margin and hurt account health. Most are caused by expectation mismatch - and the fix is a clearer, more honest listing, not a lower price.

Fix sizing first

Wrong size is the top return reason in fashion and many categories. Add an accurate size chart, real measurements, and a scale image. Tell buyers exactly what they will receive.

Make images honest, not just pretty

Over-edited photos raise returns when the product looks different in hand. Show true colour, texture, and scale. See the product photography tips.

Complete the specs buyers check

Exact dimensionsMaterial / fabricQuantity / packCompatibilityCare instructionsWhat is included

Use returns and reviews as data

Read your return reasons and reviews, find the repeated complaint, and fix that exact gap on the listing. Do it fast with the review analyzer, then re-check with the Listing Score tool.

Cut RTO with clarity and packaging

Reduce refused deliveries with realistic delivery timelines, accurate stock, and sturdy packaging that arrives undamaged. Damaged-in-transit is a preventable return.

Model the real cost of returns

A return is not just a refund - it is shipping both ways, handling, and sometimes lost stock. Factor it into pricing with the ROI Planner so discounts do not wipe out your margin.

Frequently asked questions

Biggest cause of returns?

Expectation mismatch - wrong size, colour, or perceived quality. Accurate sizing, honest images, and clear specs prevent most of it.

How do I lower RTO?

Clear delivery timelines, accurate stock, and listings that set correct expectations so buyers do not refuse on delivery.

Do returns affect ranking?

High returns hurt account health, margin, and visibility. Lowering them helps profit and long-term ranking.

Where do I start?

Fix the single most-returned product's listing first - usually a sizing or specification gap.
