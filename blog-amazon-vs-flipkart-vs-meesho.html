Amazon vs Flipkart vs Meesho: where should you sell?

There is no single best marketplace - only the best fit for your product, budget, and audience. Here is how the three big Indian platforms compare.

Meesho - easiest to start, price-driven

Great for fashion, home, and low-to-mid-price daily products. Simple onboarding and a highly price-sensitive audience. Win with clear photos, simple copy, and sharp pricing. See Meesho listing help.

Amazon - premium reach and rich tools

Broad, higher-intent audience and the most seller tooling (A+ Content, deep PPC, brand registry). More competitive and detail-heavy, but strong for quality products and brands. See Amazon listing help.

Flipkart - strong for value-focused India

Big reach with Indian value shoppers, fast-scanning listings, and strong festive events. Win with clear highlights and specifications. See Flipkart listing help.
