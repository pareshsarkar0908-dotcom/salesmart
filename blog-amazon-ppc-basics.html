Amazon PPC for beginners: Sponsored Products in India.

Ads can buy the early visibility organic ranking takes months to earn - but only if you understand the basics. Here is Amazon PPC explained simply for new Indian sellers.

What PPC actually is

PPC means you bid to appear in search results and pay only when someone clicks. Sponsored Products - ads that look like normal listings - is where every beginner should start.

Auto vs manual campaigns

Auto campaigns let Amazon choose keywords - great for discovering what buyers actually search. Manual campaigns let you bid on specific keywords you have chosen. Run one of each on your best listing.

Understand ACoS

ACoS is ad spend divided by ad sales. If your margin is 30%, keep ACoS below 30% to stay profitable. Model your true margin first with the ROI Planner so you know your break-even.

Keyword match types

Ads amplify a good listing - not a bad one

PPC sends traffic, but your listing does the converting. Fix the title, bullets, images, and price first with the Listing Generator and Listing Score tool, or you will pay for clicks that never buy.

A simple starter plan

  1. Pick one strong, well-optimised listing.
  2. Run one auto + one manual campaign with a small daily budget.
  3. After 1-2 weeks, add converting search terms as exact keywords.
  4. Add non-converting terms as negatives.
  5. Scale budget only on profitable keywords.

Frequently asked questions

What is Amazon PPC?

Pay-per-click ads where you bid to appear in search and pay only on clicks. Sponsored Products is the beginner type.

What is a good ACoS?

Below your product margin. If margin is 30%, keep ACoS under 30% to stay profitable.

How much should I spend?

Start with a small daily budget on your best listing; scale only what converts profitably.

Do I need ads to rank?

No, but early ad sales can boost velocity and help organic ranking on a well-built listing.
