How the calculator works

Commission

A percentage of your selling price by marketplace and category. As of 2026, Amazon and Flipkart charge 0% commission under ₹1,000, Meesho charges 0% on everything, and Flipkart fashion is always 0% — so shipping, fixed fees, GST and returns often matter more than commission.

Shipping & zone

Forward shipping rises with weight and distance — Local is cheapest, National the most. Heavier parcels cost more per slab.

GST on fees

Marketplaces charge 18% GST on their commission and shipping fees, which is deducted from your settlement.

RTO / returns

A returned order loses forward and reverse shipping with no sale. Toggle RTO to see how returns eat into profit — the top hidden cost for Indian sellers.
