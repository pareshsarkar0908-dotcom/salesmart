Meesho catalog upload: a step-by-step guide for new sellers.

Your first Meesho catalog decides how fast you start getting orders. This guide walks through every step - and the mistakes that get catalogs rejected.

Before you upload: what you need

Supplier accountBank detailsGSTIN (if required for your category)Clean product photosPrice and stock countProduct attributes

Step 1: Pick the correct category

Category controls which attributes and filters your product appears under. Choosing the wrong one buries your listing. Match the exact product type, not a broad guess.

Step 2: Upload clean, compliant images

Use well-lit photos on a plain background, no watermarks, logos, or text overlays. Show the product clearly from the angles a buyer needs. Bad images are the top rejection reason.

Step 3: Write a simple, clear title and details

Meesho buyers compare fast, so keep the title plain: product, material, key feature, and variant. Fill fabric, size, colour, and pack fields accurately. Draft the copy quickly with the Meesho listing generator.

Step 4: Price for the Meesho buyer

Meesho shoppers are price-sensitive and compare many similar products. Check competitors, then confirm your margin survives shipping and returns using the ROI Planner.

Step 5: Single vs bulk upload

List your first few products one by one to learn the fields. Once you are comfortable, use the bulk catalog file to add many products consistently and save time.

Common rejection fixes

If a catalog is rejected, read the exact flagged field: replace watermarked images, correct the category, complete mandatory attributes, remove misleading claims, and fix price or stock mismatches. Then re-submit.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need GST to sell on Meesho?

Some categories allow non-GST selling, but many need a GSTIN. Check the current requirement for your category in the supplier panel.

Why does Meesho reject catalogs?

Poor or watermarked images, wrong category, missing attributes, misleading titles, or price/stock mismatches. Fix the flagged field and re-submit.

Single or bulk upload?

Single while learning the fields, then bulk once confident to list many products quickly and consistently.

How do I get more Meesho orders?

Clear images, accurate attributes, competitive price, fast dispatch, and genuine ratings. Refresh weak listings monthly.
