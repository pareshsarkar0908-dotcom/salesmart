Shopify store vs marketplace: where should Indian sellers sell?

Marketplaces hand you shoppers but take control and margin. An own store gives control and margin but no built-in traffic. Here is how to decide - and why the answer is often "both".

Marketplaces: instant traffic, less control

Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho bring millions of ready buyers, so you start selling fast. The trade-off is commission and fees, fierce price competition, limited branding, and rules you must follow.

Shopify: control and margin, but you bring traffic

Your own store means full control of design, branding, data, and better margins with no marketplace commission. But there are no built-in shoppers - you drive every visit with SEO, ads, social, and content.

How to choose

Need sales fast? MarketplaceBuilding a brand? Own storeNo marketing budget yet? MarketplaceWant customer data? Own storeThin margins? Own store helpsWant both reach + brand? Do both

Why most brands do both

Marketplaces are for discovery and volume; your store is for margin, branding, and repeat customers. Run marketplaces to be found, then bring loyal buyers to your own store over time.

Frequently asked questions

Shopify or marketplace?

Marketplaces for instant traffic and less control; Shopify for control, branding, and margin but you bring traffic.

Free traffic on Shopify?

No built-in shoppers - you drive traffic via SEO, ads, social, and content.

Can I do both?

Yes - marketplaces for reach, own store for brand and margin, with consistent product facts.

Which first for a new seller?

Usually a marketplace for early sales, then add a store as the brand grows.
