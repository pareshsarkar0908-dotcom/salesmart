7 Amazon listing mistakes costing you sales in 2026.

Most Amazon India listings do not fail because of the algorithm. They fail because the page does not answer buyer questions quickly. Here are the seven mistakes we see most often, and the exact fix for each.

1. A vague, keyword-first title

A title like "Premium Quality Steel Bottle Best" tells the buyer nothing. Lead with the product, then the variant, size or pack, and the strongest benefit. Fix: use the formula Brand + Product + Key Feature + Size/Quantity + Use Case. See Amazon title examples.

2. Bullets that list features, not benefits

"Double-wall steel" is a feature; "keeps drinks cold for 24 hours" is the benefit that sells. Fix: start each bullet with the benefit, then support it with the fact. Use the five-bullet framework.

3. Keyword stuffing

Repeating the same phrase in every field hurts readability and conversion, and it does not improve ranking. Fix: spread natural variations across the title, bullets, description, and backend keywords. Build the set with the keyword research guide.

4. Missing specifications and variant details

Buyers abandon listings that hide size, material, quantity, or compatibility. Fix: put the facts a buyer needs to decide in the first two bullets and the description. Read how to write product descriptions.

5. Ignoring what reviews are telling you

Repeated complaints about sizing or packaging are free market research. Fix: mine your reviews and competitor reviews for objections, then answer them on the page. Use the review analyzer.

6. No HSN code or GST clarity

Getting tax fields wrong causes rejected listings and compliance headaches for Indian sellers. Fix: confirm the correct HSN code and GST slab for your category before you publish, and verify it with a tax professional.

7. Publishing once and never refreshing

Listings decay as competition and search behaviour change. Fix: run a monthly audit on impressions, clicks, conversion, and new reviews. Score the page with the Listing Score tool and fix the weakest section first.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common Amazon listing mistake?

A vague title. If it does not state the product type, key variant, size or pack, and main benefit, buyers scroll past and the listing ranks poorly.

How many bullet points should I use?

Five. Each should answer one buyer question - benefit, material, size or compatibility, care or use, and trust - not repeat features.

Does keyword stuffing help ranking?

No. It hurts readability and conversion, and modern search rewards relevance and sales, not repetition.

How often should I update a listing?

Review it monthly using impressions, clicks, conversion, and new customer questions, and refresh the weakest section.
