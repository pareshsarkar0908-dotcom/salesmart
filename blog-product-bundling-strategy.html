Product bundling strategy to increase order value.

Bundling is one of the easiest ways to raise average order value without new traffic. Done right, it makes buying easier and clears slow stock - done wrong, it confuses buyers and raises returns.

What bundling is - and why it works

A bundle sells related items together as one listing, usually at a small saving. It lifts order value, simplifies the buyer's decision, and can rank for "combo" and "set" searches.

Bundle types that sell

Core + accessory (bottle + sleeve)Starter kit / setMultipack of a consumableGifting comboGood-better-best tiersSeasonal / festive combo

Bundle around a real reason

The items must genuinely belong together. A buyer should think "yes, I need both" - not "why are these sold together?". Forced bundles raise returns and hurt reviews.

Price so the value is obvious - and profitable

Set the bundle below the separate-items cost, but only within your margin after fees, shipping, and returns. Check it with the ROI Planner before you publish.

Write the bundle listing clearly

State exactly what is included, quantities, and the benefit of buying together. Target combo and set keywords with the SEO Keywords tool, then build the copy with the Listing Generator.

Common bundling mistakes

Avoid hiding the per-item value, bundling a dead product just to offload it, unclear quantities, and photos that do not show every item. Confusion at checkout becomes a return later.

Frequently asked questions

What is product bundling?

Selling related items together as one listing, often at a small saving, to raise order value and simplify the purchase.

Does it help on Amazon/Flipkart?

Yes when items genuinely go together and value is clear - it can win combo searches and lift order value.

How do I price a bundle?

Below the separate-items cost, but only as far as margin allows after fees, shipping, and returns.

What should I avoid?

Forced bundles, hidden quantities, and photos that do not show every included item.
