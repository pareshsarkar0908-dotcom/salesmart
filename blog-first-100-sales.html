How to get your first 100 sales on Amazon and Flipkart.

The first 100 orders are the hardest - and the most important, because they create the reviews and ranking signals that make the next 1,000 easier. Here is a focused playbook.

1. Pick one focused product

Do not launch a scattered catalogue. Choose one product with real demand, manageable competition, and margin you can defend. Validate it with the Product Research tool.

2. Build a complete, keyword-led listing

A half-filled listing cannot rank or convert. Research keywords, then write a strong title, five bullets, description, and backend terms. Use the SEO Keywords and Listing Generator tools.

3. Price to earn early orders

Early on, momentum matters more than maximum margin. Price competitively to win first orders and reviews - but confirm you still profit with the ROI Planner.

4. Earn genuine early reviews

Deliver well, package carefully, and follow up politely. Early honest reviews build the trust that lifts conversion and ranking. Never buy fake reviews - it risks suspension.

5. Run a small, focused ad budget

Once the listing is complete and priced right, a small ad spend on your one strong listing buys early visibility and data. Ads amplify a good listing; they cannot rescue a weak one.

6. Improve weekly using data

  1. Check impressions, clicks, conversion, and returns.
  2. Read new reviews and questions for objections.
  3. Fix the single weakest part of the listing.
  4. Score the page with the Listing Score tool.
  5. Repeat - small weekly gains compound fast.

Frequently asked questions

How do new sellers get first sales?

Focused product, complete keyword-led listing, competitive price, a small ad budget, and weekly data-driven improvement.

How important are early reviews?

Very - they build trust and lift conversion and ranking. Earn them genuinely; never buy fake reviews.

Should I run ads immediately?

Only after the listing is complete and priced well. Ads amplify a good listing, not a weak one.

One product or many?

Start with one and get it right. Scale the catalogue after you understand what converts.
