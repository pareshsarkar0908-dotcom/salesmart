Prepare your listings for festive season sales in India.

Festive events like the Big Billion Days and Great Indian Festival drive a huge share of yearly sales. The sellers who win prepared their listings weeks early - here is the checklist.

Start 3-4 weeks early

New copy and images need time to get indexed, and early orders build the reviews and velocity that lift ranking during the event. Preparing late means fighting the rush with a cold listing.

Refresh keywords for seasonal intent

Buyers search differently near festivals - gifting, combos, budget, and occasion terms rise. Regenerate your keyword set with the SEO Keywords tool and weave seasonal phrases in naturally.

Update titles, bullets, and images

Add gifting and use-case angles, refresh the first image, and make sure specs are complete. Rework the copy fast with the Listing Generator, then confirm quality with the Listing Score tool.

Get stock and pricing right

Running out mid-sale kills momentum and ranking. Forecast demand, keep a buffer, and confirm your festive price still protects margin using the ROI Planner before you discount.

Do edits before, not during

Major rewrites during a live sale can reset performance signals at the worst time. Finish changes before the event; during it, focus on stock, price, and answering buyer questions quickly.

Frequently asked questions

When should I prepare?

Three to four weeks before the sale, so copy and images index and gather early reviews, and you can fix stock and pricing calmly.

Should I edit during the sale?

Do major edits before the event; mid-sale rewrites can reset signals. During it, focus on stock, price, and fast replies.

How do I rank higher in festive season?

Seasonal keywords, complete fields, gifting angles, healthy stock, competitive price, and early reviews.

Which events matter most in India?

The major Amazon and Flipkart festival sales around Dussehra and Diwali drive the biggest yearly demand spikes.
